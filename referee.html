Match record

Enter each player's contributions for this match only. Goals and assists update season totals. Yellow cards: 0–2; red cards: 0–1 (include a dismissal for a second yellow).

A team score may include own goals or goals with an unidentified scorer. Assists cannot exceed recorded player goals. If older match contributions were already entered as season totals, ask the administrator to reconcile them before adding them here.
